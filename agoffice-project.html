GIMAGRAPHY — It’s Time to Marketing
01 / OVERVIEW

In October 2024, I had the opportunity to design and facilitate a comprehensive Social Media Management Workshop under the Community Empowerment Project organised by the Dibulagala Divisional Secretariat. The session was held on 22 October 2024 at the Divisional Secretariat premises and formed part of a broader initiative aimed at strengthening local entrepreneurship through practical digital skills.

Operating under the identity GIMAGRAPHY – It’s Time to Marketing, the workshop was specifically created for individuals who had recently started businesses or were preparing to launch their own ventures. Many participants had limited prior exposure to professional social media practices, so the programme was structured to begin from foundational concepts and progressively build practical competence.

02 / PROGRAMME FOCUS & CONTENT

The workshop covered a complete introductory-to-intermediate pathway in social media management, with particular emphasis on platforms and strategies relevant to the Sri Lankan market. Key modules included:

  • Setting up and optimising Facebook Business Pages for professional use
  • Developing coherent social media strategies aligned with business goals
  • Content planning, calendar creation, and consistent posting approaches
  • Techniques for genuine audience engagement and community building
  • Practical methods for growing online visibility and converting attention into business results

Live demonstrations, real-time platform walkthroughs, and guided discussions formed the core of the delivery style, ensuring participants could immediately apply what they learned to their own businesses.

03 / COLLABORATION & SUPPORT

The success of the programme was significantly supported by the cooperation and hospitality of the Dibulagala Divisional Secretariat staff. Special appreciation is due to Manjula Jayawardena and the team, whose organisational assistance, venue arrangements, and warm facilitation created a productive and welcoming environment for all participants.

04 / IMPACT & INTENT

From one-day training to a practical digital foundation.

This initiative was designed not merely as a one-day training session, but as a practical foundation that local entrepreneurs could continue building upon independently. By focusing on realistic, locally relevant strategies rather than abstract theory, the workshop aimed to help participants develop a stronger digital presence, increase meaningful engagement, and ultimately turn social media into a sustainable growth channel for their businesses.

The project reflects my ongoing commitment to community-level capacity building, digital literacy, and the empowerment of emerging entrepreneurs through accessible, hands-on marketing education.
