GIMAGRAPHY / FIELD MEDIA PROJECT

Dimbulagala Tourism Promotion & Media Coverage

Media Coverage, Visual Documentation, Content Creation & Tourism Promotion · Dimbulagala, Polonnaruwa

GIMAGRAPHY · PROFESSIONAL MEDIA COVERAGE
01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

The Dimbulagala Tourism Promotion & Media Coverage Project was a field-based initiative focused on exploring, documenting, and promoting the tourism potential of Dimbulagala and surrounding areas of the Polonnaruwa District.

The programme was conducted under the leadership of Professor Raj Somadeva, bringing together archaeology, cultural heritage, natural landscapes, tourism, media, and local business development.

Through GIMAGRAPHY, I provided comprehensive media coverage in collaboration with DREAM VIEW HOTEL, documenting field activities, locations, experiences, tourism-related aspects, and the overall atmosphere through photography, visual content creation, and media documentation.

02 / PROJECT OBJECTIVES
  • Explore and document historically and archaeologically significant locations.
  • Observe and document the natural beauty and unique landscapes of the Dimbulagala region.
  • Create visual content that can contribute to tourism promotion.
  • Provide professional media coverage for the programme.
  • Support the promotional visibility of DREAM VIEW HOTEL.
  • Capture authentic field experiences through photography and visual storytelling.
  • Develop practical exposure to professional media and field documentation.
03 / GIMAGRAPHY'S ROLE
  • Planning and conducting field-based media coverage.
  • Photography and visual documentation.
  • Capturing tourism-oriented photographs and visual content.
  • Documenting archaeological and culturally significant locations.
  • Capturing natural landscapes and environmental beauty.
  • Creating visual stories around the field experience.
  • Supporting the promotional visibility of DREAM VIEW HOTEL.
  • Organising visual material for future digital and promotional use.
04 / ARCHAEOLOGY & CULTURAL EXPLORATION

The field visits provided direct exposure to archaeological and historically significant locations within the Dimbulagala region. The experience strengthened my understanding of Sri Lanka's archaeological and cultural heritage and the importance of responsible visual storytelling when documenting places with historical, archaeological, and cultural significance.

The programme also helped connect historical sites, surrounding landscapes, local culture, and tourism into a broader understanding of destination identity.

05 / LEARNING FROM PROFESSOR RAJ SOMADEVA

A particularly valuable part of the project was the opportunity to interact with and learn from Professor Raj Somadeva during the field programme. His academic and field experience provided valuable guidance for understanding archaeological and historical locations from a more informed perspective.

The experience strengthened the relationship between Archaeology → Heritage → Environment → Tourism → Storytelling.

06 / NATURAL ENVIRONMENT & TOURISM

Dimbulagala's landscapes, natural formations, surroundings, and scenic atmosphere created valuable opportunities for destination-focused visual storytelling. Through GIMAGRAPHY, these elements were documented to present Dimbulagala as a destination with natural, cultural, and experiential tourism potential.

The project helped develop a more complete approach to tourism photography: communicating the experience and identity of a destination, not simply photographing a location.

07 / PROFESSIONAL MEDIA EXPOSURE

Working alongside experienced media professionals provided practical insight into field coverage, communication, moment selection, documentation workflows, and professional collaboration.

These interactions strengthened my confidence in communicating and collaborating with professionals from different areas of the media and tourism sectors.

08 / PROJECT IMPACT

Connecting heritage, tourism, business and storytelling through visual media.

The project contributed to the visual documentation and promotional storytelling of Dimbulagala and surrounding areas of the Polonnaruwa District. The programme's activities, locations, natural surroundings, professional interactions, and tourism-related experiences were documented for digital and promotional purposes.

More importantly, the experience demonstrated how creative media can connect heritage, tourism, business, and storytelling while helping communicate the value of Sri Lanka's historical and natural destinations to wider audiences.

The collaboration with DREAM VIEW HOTEL also provided practical exposure to working with a business stakeholder while maintaining the quality and professionalism expected from a media and creative service provider.

09 / PERSONAL REFLECTION

This project became one of the most valuable field experiences I have gained through GIMAGRAPHY. Exploring Dimbulagala while learning about its archaeological and natural significance gave me a different perspective on content creation.

I learned that effective media coverage is not simply about capturing visually attractive photographs; it is about understanding the story, context, people, environment, and purpose behind what is being documented.

The experience strengthened my belief that digital media can be used not only for commercial promotion, but also as a powerful tool for cultural preservation, tourism awareness, knowledge sharing, and destination promotion.
