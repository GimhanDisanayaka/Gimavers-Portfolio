GIMAGRAPHY / DIGITAL CAMPAIGN

Thalanjalee Ves Mangalya

Social Media Management & Content Creation · Thalanjalee Dancing Academy

OFFICIAL SOCIAL MEDIA MANAGER & CONTENT CREATOR
01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

In this project, I served as the Official Social Media Manager and Content Creator for Thalanjalee Dancing Academy, leading the complete digital campaign for their annual Ves Mangalya cultural event.

The goal was to elevate a traditional Sri Lankan dance celebration into a powerful online presence that would increase awareness, drive engagement, and bring cultural heritage to a wider digital audience.

From initial concept development through final content delivery, I managed the full social media ecosystem for the event, including strategic planning, content creation, community interaction, and continuous campaign optimisation.

02 / MY ROLE & RESPONSIBILITIES
  • Content Strategy — Developed a cohesive content plan aligned with the cultural significance of Ves Mangalya while optimising for modern social media algorithms and audience behaviour.
  • Video Production — Planned, shot, and edited high-quality video content capturing performances, behind-the-scenes moments, interviews, and event highlights.
  • Social Media Management — Handled day-to-day page management, posting schedules, audience interaction, and overall platform growth.
  • Copywriting — Crafted culturally respectful yet engaging captions, promotional texts, and storytelling content in both Sinhala and English contexts.
  • Community Engagement — Actively built and nurtured online conversations, responded to supporters, and strengthened the academy’s digital community.
  • Campaign Promotion — Executed targeted promotional efforts to increase event visibility and drive both online and offline participation.
03 / DIGITAL CAMPAIGN APPROACH

The campaign was designed around a balance between cultural authenticity and contemporary digital communication. Content was structured to showcase the artistic quality of the performances while also giving audiences access to the people, preparation, atmosphere, and story behind the event.

The strategy connected event storytelling, video production, community interaction, promotional communication, and ongoing page management into one consistent digital campaign.

The result was a social presence designed not simply to promote an event, but to make Sri Lankan dance heritage more visible, accessible, and engaging for online audiences.

04 / RESULTS & IMPACT

Amplifying authentic cultural art through strategic digital storytelling.

100+ Original content pieces created
100K+ People reached
10K+ Engagements generated
100% Dedicated campaign presence maintained

The campaign delivered strong measurable outcomes, including 100+ original content pieces, 50K+ people reached, and 10K+ engagements, while maintaining a consistent, high-quality digital presence throughout the campaign period.

Beyond the numbers, the project successfully bridged traditional cultural performance with modern digital storytelling. It helped position Thalanjalee Dancing Academy as a vibrant and accessible cultural institution in the online space while fostering greater public appreciation for Sri Lankan dance heritage.

This collaboration remains one of the more meaningful projects in my portfolio — combining strategic social media expertise with genuine respect for cultural tradition, and demonstrating how thoughtful digital communication can amplify authentic local art forms.
