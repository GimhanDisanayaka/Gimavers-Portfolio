Community Empowerment Initiative · Dimbulagala Divisional Secretariat · 2023

GIMAGRAPHY — Strategic Brand Development
01 / OVERVIEW

In 2023, under the patronage of the Dimbulagala Divisional Secretariat, I led a large-scale Brand Design Project as part of a community empowerment programme. The initiative focused on supporting selected local entrepreneurs and small businesses by providing complete professional branding solutions that would help them establish a strong visual identity and compete more effectively in both physical and digital markets.

Operating through GIMAGRAPHY, the project delivered end-to-end brand development services to more than 25 carefully selected businesses. Many participants were newly established or in the early stages of formalising their operations and lacked professional branding assets.

02 / SCOPE OF WORK

Each participating business received a comprehensive branding package designed to create a cohesive, practical, and market-ready visual presence.

  • Custom logo design and complete brand identity systems
  • Product label and packaging design for food and spice-based products
  • Business card design and supporting promotional materials
  • Facebook page design and social media visual templates
  • Creative direction and consultation for brand consistency
03 / APPROACH & TOOLS

Each brand was developed around the nature of the business, local market preferences, and the need for clear, professional communication. The approach balanced cultural relevance with modern visual standards so that the brands could feel authentic to Sri Lankan consumers while remaining polished and trustworthy.

04 / PROJECT IMPACT

Turning local business ideas into recognisable, trustworthy brands.

25+ Local businesses directly supported
20+ Distinct brand identities and asset systems created

The initiative directly supported over 25 local businesses, resulting in the creation of more than 20 distinct brand identities and associated assets. Participants gained professional visual systems that helped them appear more credible to customers, improve product presentation, and build stronger recognition both offline and online.

By providing these foundational branding elements free of commercial barriers, the project contributed to tangible improvements in market presence for emerging entrepreneurs in the Dimbulagala area.

This project stands as one of the more substantial community-focused branding efforts I have undertaken, demonstrating the power of strategic design in turning local business ideas into recognisable and trustworthy brands.
