Thalanjalee Dancing Academy — CIDFA 2024

Social Media Management · Content Creation · Campaign Strategy

SOCIAL MEDIA MANAGER & CONTENT CREATOR
01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

In 2024, I continued my role as Social Media Manager and Content Creator for Thalanjalee Dancing Academy during their participation in the Colombo International Dance Festival (CIDFA).

This project represented a significant chapter in an ongoing collaboration, where strategic digital communication played a central role in elevating the academy’s visibility, strengthening public support, and contributing to notable achievements at one of Sri Lanka’s prominent national dance platforms.

My responsibility was to build and sustain a compelling online presence that amplified the academy’s journey, engaged audiences, and supported the broader promotional efforts surrounding the festival.

02 / SCOPE OF WORK
  • Content & Engagement Strategy — Developed a focused strategy tailored to the festival timeline.
  • Promotional Content — Created high-quality videos and visual assets capturing the energy, skill, and spirit of the academy.
  • Ongoing Content — Produced reels, posts, stories, and updates to maintain momentum and audience interest.
  • Social Media Management — Managed day-to-day publishing, community interaction, and real-time engagement.
  • Copywriting — Crafted culturally respectful, platform-optimised copy for traditional and younger digital audiences.
  • Campaign Narrative — Supported a clear, authentic, and compelling story throughout the festival journey.
03 / FESTIVAL RECOGNITION

The academy’s efforts were recognised with multiple awards at the Colombo International Dance Festival 2024. The awarding ceremony was held at Sugathadasa Stadium.

05 / PROJECT SIGNIFICANCE

The digital efforts delivered strong measurable outcomes, with 80+ original content pieces, 150K+ people reached, and 50K+ engagements across platforms.

This collaboration stands as a clear example of how thoughtful content strategy and consistent social media management can support cultural institutions in achieving greater recognition. By combining creative storytelling with disciplined execution, the campaign helped translate the academy’s on-stage excellence into wider public awareness and tangible festival success.

It remains a project I am particularly proud of — bringing together cultural heritage, performance excellence, and modern digital communication to deliver meaningful results.
